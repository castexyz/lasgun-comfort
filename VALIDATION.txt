REVIEW / LIMITS
New pistol audio uses the existing dry-source gain mechanism. No global audio
settings are changed. Other sounds overlapping on the same weapon source can
briefly inherit reduced gain. Reverb and separate tails/extras are not separately
controlled. Native gain failures retain original firing events; failed cleanup
can require disabling the control and restarting the game.
Muzzle pre-spawn suppression targets exact energy-weapon resources; mixed FX
with casings or a secondary muzzle retain the original method and use the
existing World particle hook. Other particle paths can leave initial bursts or
embedded lights. Original beam shaders themselves can remain bright.
New glow controls affect matching teammates' locally rendered beams too; shared
laser impacts remain shared. No extra runtime dependency. Existing rifle gain
curve and the beta3 Helbore next-charge fix are retained. Credits and AI disclosure
kept in the separate documentation folder. Multiplayer and mod combinations
remain unverified. Owner confirms overall in-game operation.
No dedicated security review was requested or performed.
